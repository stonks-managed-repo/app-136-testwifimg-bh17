How to buy

three moves

Get a TON wallet

Install a wallet that supports TON and supports connecting to dapps (TonConnect). Write your seed phrase down on actual paper. Never paste it into a website — including this one.

wallet → ready

Load it with TON or GRAM

Send TON (or GRAM) to your new wallet address from an exchange or another wallet. Keep a little extra TON on top for network fees — transactions on TON are cheap, but not free.

balance > 0

Buy on Stonks

Open app.stonks.cash, connect your wallet, find $testwifimg, enter the amount, and confirm the transaction in your wallet. The tokens land in the same wallet.

confirm → done

Always check that you're on the real Stonks domain and that the token you pick is testwifimg. Crypto is volatile; only use what you can afford to lose.
